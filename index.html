On friction

Friction doesn’t get removed. It gets placed. Some of it is parasitic — it heats and costs. Some of it is structural: it’s the only reason force transmits at all. Most of this work is telling them apart.

I’ve done this in an industrial plant, in my own kitchen, and in a system that holds my own life. Nobody asked me to. It’s what the work turned out to need.
