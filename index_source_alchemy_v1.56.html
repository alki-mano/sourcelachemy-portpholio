01 · The Method

From first thought
to living system.

We all carry more ideas than we ever ship. The gap isn't creativity — it's the absence of a reliable path from raw to real. Source Alchemy is a five-stage cycle built and tested making a food company from zero.

Each stage transforms something. Each cycle ends with a handoff — a living record that carries the project forward.

01
✏
Source
Paper, voice, whiteboard. The raw material — no filter, no format. Real thinking happens here, before any tool opens.
02
◎
Channel
Voice-to-text, transcription, brain dump. Getting what lives in your head out — and into a form that can be worked with.
03
⬡
Structure
AI conversation, pattern recognition, decision mapping. Where chaos finds its form — without losing its character.
04
✦
Output
A design, an SOP, a financial model, a strategy. Something real — passable to a human, readable by a machine.
05
↺
Iterate
Close the loop. Refine. Decide what changed. Each cycle starts from a known state — not from zero.
⬦ Running through every stage
Handoff — The living document. It builds as you work. At any moment, it holds everything a person or an AI needs to continue without starting over.

Not the music.

Each tool has one role in the cycle. Nothing added to feel complete — only what earns its place.

LLM
